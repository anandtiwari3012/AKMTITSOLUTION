Detailed Product Description

The Angle EXACT 12V-12-400 cordless angle screwdriver is designed for industrial assembly operations requiring high precision fastening and repeatable torque performance.

Its compact angle-head design enables operators to access confined spaces where standard screwdrivers cannot operate efficiently. This makes it suitable for automotive production lines, electronics manufacturing, appliance assembly, and precision industrial applications.

Built with torque-controlled shutoff technology, the tool helps reduce fastening errors, improves process consistency, and minimizes costly rework during production.

The lightweight ergonomic construction helps reduce operator fatigue during long production cycles while maintaining fastening accuracy and reliability.
